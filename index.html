How it works

From idea to your desk in four steps.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Tell us what you need, what gear it's for and any measurements or photos.

  2. 02

    Design together

    We sketch and model it with you, adjusting until the fit and look are right.

  3. 03

    Print & finish

    We print your piece, then check it and clean it up by hand.

  4. 04

    Delivered

    Your accessory arrives ready to use, built for your setup alone.

About us

Small studio, personal service.

PurelyPrintellectual LLC is a small design-and-print studio. Instead of selling off-the-shelf parts, we work directly with each client to design custom PC and desk accessories, such as headphone mounts, organizers and stands, made to fit the gear they already own.

Every order starts with a conversation, so you're talking to the people who design and print your piece.
